# Vocoro Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-05-15
**Last updated:** 2026-05-15

Vocoro ("we", "us", "the app") is a vocabulary learning game for children ages 6–13. We have designed Vocoro to be **fully offline** and to collect **no personal data**. This policy explains what that means in practice.

---

## 1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Vocoro mobile app on iOS or Android, including children and the parents or guardians who set up the app for them.

Because Vocoro is intended for children under 13, we follow the requirements of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines, and the Google Play Families Policy.

---

## 2. What information we collect

**We do not collect any personal information.** Specifically:

- We do **not** ask for an email address, phone number, real name, date of birth, address, or any other contact information.
- We do **not** create user accounts or require sign-in.
- We do **not** use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.
- We do **not** transmit data to any server. Vocoro has no backend.
- We do **not** access the device's camera, microphone, contacts, photos, location, or any other system permission beyond what is required for the app to run.

The only inputs the app accepts from the user are:

- A player **display name** (this can be a nickname, initial, or anything the child chooses) — stored only on the device.
- An **age band** selection (6–8, 9–11, or 12–13) — used solely to tune in-app difficulty, stored only on the device.
- An **avatar** and **theme** preference — stored only on the device.

These values never leave the device.

---

## 3. What information is stored on the device

To make the game work between sessions, Vocoro stores the following **on the device only**:

- The player's display name, age band, avatar, and theme choice.
- Game progress: current world, current level, mastery levels per word, stars, keys, streak count.
- Settings: music on/off, voice on/off, text size, difficulty preference.
- A randomly generated number ("word order seed") used to shuffle which words appear in which levels for that player.

This information is stored in a local SQLite database and in the device's standard app preferences. It is included in standard device backups (e.g., iCloud Backup, Google One Backup) if the user or parent has enabled those backups at the operating-system level. We do not control or access those backups.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data from the device.

---

## 4. Speech (text-to-speech)

Vocoro can speak words aloud using the device's built-in text-to-speech engine (Apple's `AVSpeechSynthesizer` on iOS, Android's `TextToSpeech` on Android). The text passed to the engine is the vocabulary word, its kid-friendly meaning, or its memory cue — all of which are pre-loaded content shipped with the app.

Some devices may use a cloud-based voice provided by the operating system itself. That behavior is controlled by the device's settings, not by Vocoro. We do not send any user-generated content to a speech service.

---

## 5. Ads, in-app purchases, and third parties

- Vocoro contains **no advertising** of any kind.
- Vocoro contains **no in-app purchases**.
- Vocoro contains **no links to social media** or external websites inside the child-facing experience.
- Vocoro does **not** share data with any third party, because no data leaves the device.

---

## 6. Parent gate

Settings, parent dashboard, and any future configuration options are placed behind a simple parent gate (a math question or comparable challenge) so that young children cannot accidentally change them.

---

## 7. Parental rights

Because Vocoro stores no information off the device and creates no user account, there is nothing for us to access, export, correct, or delete on a remote server. Parents who wish to delete their child's progress can do so at any time by:

1. Uninstalling Vocoro from the device, or
2. Clearing the app's storage from the device's system settings.

Either action permanently removes all local data created by the app.

---

## 8. Changes to this policy

If we ever change Vocoro to collect, transmit, or share any information, we will update this policy, change the "Last updated" date above, and surface a clear notice inside the app before the change takes effect. We will not retroactively apply a new policy to data that was created under the current policy.
